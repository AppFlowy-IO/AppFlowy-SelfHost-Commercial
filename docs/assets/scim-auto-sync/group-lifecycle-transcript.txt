Idle waits are removed; duration is not a sync-latency measurement. The new group was included in the existing provider’s Group filters during setup, outside this recording. No manual full sync or direct AppFlowy membership edit was used. The separate Retry sync exercise is shown in retry-group-sync.mp4.
